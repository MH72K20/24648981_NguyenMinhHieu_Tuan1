Body Systems LSM400

Linear Smith Machine

Designed for both serious fitness users and beginners, the LSM400's vertical design provides all safety and control associated with Smith Machines while maximizing natural gravity resistance.

Smooth Smith Action

Bar rated at 800 lb. capacity slides on ultra-smooth Teflon bushings alongside solid steel guide rods for maximum muscle utilization.

Proper 7° Lifting Angle

20 Safety Lock Points

Integrated lockout positions ensure a safe, solid stop at any moment of your repetition without needing a workout partner.

Rugged Construction

Built with heavy 12-gauge steel frames and 7-gauge reinforcing plates designed for lifetimes of intensive training.

Comprehensive lifetime coverage on every single part. We repair or replace any component guarantee.
